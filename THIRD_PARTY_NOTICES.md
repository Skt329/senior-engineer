## Karpathy-inspired coding guidelines

Section 8 of `context/core-rules.md` adapts, in new wording, the ideas of the andrej-karpathy-skills project by forrestchang (also published under the multica-ai organization on GitHub), which is released under the MIT License. That project distills Andrej Karpathy's public observations about common mistakes made by coding models. No text from the project is copied here.
